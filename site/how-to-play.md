# How to play Frozen Bubble

Frozen Bubble is an arcade puzzle game about aiming. A cluster of coloured
bubbles hangs from the top of a narrow well, and a penguin at the bottom works
a launcher that fires one bubble at a time. Your job is to clear the cluster
before it reaches the bottom. This page covers the rules of the 1-player game
and the controls on every platform; the [strategy guide](../strategy/) goes
further into how to play well, and [multiplayer](../multiplayer/) explains
what changes when you play against other people.

![A 1-player game in progress](../screenshots/modern-gameplay.jpg)

## The goal

Every level starts with a pattern of bubbles stuck to the ceiling. You win the
level when the last bubble is gone. You lose when any bubble sits below the
line near the bottom of the well, just above the launcher. There is no time
limit on a level as a whole, but there is a limit on how long you can wait
between shots (see *Hurry* below), and the ceiling itself keeps coming down.

## Popping bubbles

The bubble in the launcher flies in a straight line in the direction you aim
it, bounces off the side walls, and sticks to the first bubble (or the ceiling)
it touches. If it lands touching two or more bubbles of its own colour, so that
three or more of that colour are now connected, the whole connected group pops.

Popping is only half of it. Any bubbles that were hanging from the group you
just popped, and are no longer connected to the ceiling through other bubbles,
fall off the board. Those dropped bubbles are worth more than popped ones, and
a single well-placed shot that cuts a big cluster loose can clear half the
screen.

A bubble that touches no matching group simply sticks where it lands. That is
not always a wasted shot: placing bubbles deliberately, to build a group you
will pop later, is a large part of the game.

## The next bubble and Swap

Below the launcher you can always see the **next** bubble, the one that will
be loaded after you fire. Its colour is chosen from the colours still on the
board, so once a colour is cleared completely it stops coming.

This version adds a pocket beside the next bubble, called **Swap**. Press the
Swap button when the pocket is empty and the loaded bubble goes into it, the
next bubble moves up into the launcher, and nothing is fired. Press Swap again
later and the pocketed bubble is fired in place of the loaded one, which goes
into the pocket in turn. Swap lets you hold on to a colour you know you will
need, or get rid of one that has no good place to go. The pocket empties at
the start of each level. The game's own **How to play** page, in the 1-player
menu, shows the launcher, the next bubble and the pocket labelled with your
own controls.

## The ceiling comes down

In the 1-player game the ceiling is pushed down by one row after every nine
shots, taking every bubble with it. A row of lights warns you as it gets close:
they speed up on the last shot before it moves. The further down the ceiling
gets, the less room you have, so clearing a level quickly matters even before
you think about points.

## Hurry

You cannot wait forever. If you hold a shot too long a **HURRY!** sign appears,
and if you still do not fire the launcher fires on its own in whatever
direction it is pointing. In the 100-level campaign the warning comes after
about eight seconds and the forced shot at about ten and a half; in random
levels, multiplayer training and every multiplayer game both come sooner, at
about five and seven and a half seconds.

## Points and the time bonus

The original game only counted how far you got. This version also keeps
points in 1-player games:

- 10 points for each bubble popped,
- 20 points for each bubble dropped,
- a multiplier when one shot sets off more than one pop,
- and a time bonus for clearing a level fast: 5,000 points for a clear within
  five seconds, 84 points less for every second after that, and nothing from
  65 seconds on.

A "+N" floats up from where each scoring shot lands, and the level-cleared
card shows how long the level took and the bonus it earned. Losing a life sets
your score back to zero, which is why the online board for points records the
best single life rather than the run.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard (player 1) | Gamepad | Mouse | Touch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aim | Left / Right arrows | D-pad or stick | Point | Touch the board |
| Fire | Up arrow | A | Left click | Release |
| Centre the launcher | Down arrow | | | |
| Swap | Right Shift | X | Right click | Tap below the board |
| Pause | P | Start | Pause button | Pause button |
| Menu | | | Menu button | Menu button |

Mouse and touch aim are switched on in the settings. Every key can be changed
under **CONTROLS & SETTINGS**, or during a game from the menu button at the
top left, which also has sound and music switches and a way back to the title
screen. On a phone or tablet, hold the device sideways: the game is drawn for a
wide screen.
