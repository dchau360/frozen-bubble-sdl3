# About and contact

This site is the home of **Frozen Bubble: Battle Royale**, a free,
open-source port of the classic Linux puzzle game Frozen Bubble to SDL3. It
runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and in a web browser, and keeps the
original game's levels, artwork, music and network protocol while adding
online rooms of up to twenty players, accounts, online highscores and weekly
rankings. This page says who is behind it and how to get in touch.

## Who makes it

The port is maintained by one independent developer, with contributions from
anyone who sends a fix through GitHub. It is a fan project: it is not made or
endorsed by the Frozen-Bubble Team, who wrote the original game in 2002 and
whose artwork and music it uses. The [history](../history/) page tells that
story and credits them; the full list of contributors to the original is in
[AUTHORS](https://github.com/dchau360/frozen-bubble-sdl3/blob/main/AUTHORS).

The Android version on Google Play is published by **Solina AI LLC**, a
Texas limited liability company. The company makes no claim over the
original game's code, artwork or music.

## What this site is for

The pages here are written for players:

- [How to play](../how-to-play/) explains the rules, from aiming and banking
  shots to what makes bubbles fall.
- The [strategy guide](../strategy/) covers the habits that clear levels
  faster and win multiplayer rounds.
- The [multiplayer guide](../multiplayer/) explains online rooms, bots,
  teams and local games on one screen.
- [Game modes and settings](../modes/) lists every mode and option.
- The [FAQ](../faq/) answers questions about highscores, accounts and
  devices.
- [Release notes](../news/) list what changed in each version.
- The [highscores](../scores/) and [weekly rankings](../weekly/) boards are
  live, read straight from the game's server.

The site has no accounts, comments or sign-in of its own. What the game and
the site store is described in the [privacy policy](../privacy/).

## Free software

The game is released under the GNU General Public License, version 2, like
the original. Its complete source, the server included, is public at
[github.com/dchau360/frozen-bubble-sdl3](https://github.com/dchau360/frozen-bubble-sdl3),
and anyone may build it, change it or run their own server. Builds for every
platform are on the
[releases page](https://github.com/dchau360/frozen-bubble-sdl3/releases).

## Contact

There is no support email address. Questions, bug reports, feature requests,
and privacy or data-removal requests all go to the public issue tracker,
which the maintainer reads:
[github.com/dchau360/frozen-bubble-sdl3/issues](https://github.com/dchau360/frozen-bubble-sdl3/issues).
Using the tracker keeps answers visible to the next player with the same
question. Please do not post your account code there or anywhere else: it
is the only key to your account.

For a chat with other players, or to find someone for a game, join the
community [Discord server](https://discord.gg/uE4dq8fqGW); its #net-games
channel shows the results of online rounds as they are played.
